IRCReplay — IRC Log Viewer & Stats Generator

Upload your old mIRC, XChat or HexChat log files and relive the conversation. IRCReplay is a free, browser-based IRC log viewer and statistics generator — the modern successor to mIRCStats.

Free forever

Full IRC log viewer with Instant and Replay playback modes, multi-file merge, a complete stats engine (top chatters, hourly activity, word frequency, URLs shared), HTML and PDF export, and three themes including a classic mIRC-style interface. Your logs never leave your device — all parsing happens in your browser.

IRCReplay Pro

Pro adds cloud session storage, public share links for your channel stats, and extended visualisations: activity heatmap, top chatters over time, word cloud, emoji stats, connections map, conversation starters and channel mood over time. From £3.49/month or £49.99 lifetime.

Supported formats

mIRC (binary and plain text), XChat and HexChat, with multi-file merge and automatic format detection.
